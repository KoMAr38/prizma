Prizma privacy policy

Last updated 17 October 2026.

Prizma is a presentation app that runs on your own computer. It does not collect, store or send any personal data.

  • Your presentations stay on your computer, in the folders where you save them. Prizma keeps a recovery copy of open work in its own app folder on the same computer.
  • Prizma has no account, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking.
  • Prizma does not connect to the internet. When you click a link in a presentation, it opens in your web browser, which then follows that website's own policy.
  • Purchases and trials go through the Microsoft Store. Microsoft handles payment under its own privacy statement. Prizma's author does not receive your payment details.
