What you can do with it

Open your PowerPoint files

Prizma opens and saves .pptx, .ppsx and .potx, so you can keep working with decks other people send you. It also opens PDFs, and older .ppt files when LibreOffice is installed.

Change the whole look in one click

Pick another theme on the Design tab and every slide follows: colors, fonts and backgrounds. Ten themes come with the app.

Morph, transitions and animations

Duplicate a slide, move things around, choose Morph, and Prizma animates the difference. Bring in points one at a time with entrance animations.

Share it the way you need

Export to PDF or pictures, or save as a PowerPoint file. Presenter view shows your notes, the next slide and a timer, and you can draw on slides while you present.

Questions

Does it work with PowerPoint files?
Yes. Prizma opens and saves .pptx, .pptm, .ppsx and .potx files. Very complex decks can look slightly different, as with any presentation app that is not PowerPoint.
Do I need an account or an internet connection?
No. Prizma runs on your computer and works offline. It has no account and sends no data anywhere.
Which computers does it run on?
Windows 10 and Windows 11, 64-bit.
What happens when the trial ends?
Prizma stops opening until you buy it from the Microsoft Store. Your files stay where they are and open in any app that reads PowerPoint files.
